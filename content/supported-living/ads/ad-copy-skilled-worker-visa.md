## Platform Notes

### Facebook / Instagram
- Primary platform. Run all variants. Carousel format works well — slide 1: headline, slide 2: "three models" (own/don't work, own/work 20hrs, own/full after ILR), slide 3: the numbers, slide 4: CTA.
- Retarget landing page visitors with Ad 7D (salary comparison) and Ad 7G (5-year calculation).

### WhatsApp
- Ad 7F is specifically designed to be shared in group chats. Create as a shareable graphic/infographic rather than a paid ad.
- Consider creating a WhatsApp broadcast template for direct outreach.

### TikTok
- Video format. Script the "revelation" angle: "If you're a nurse on a Skilled Worker visa, nobody told you this..." — then reveal the three ownership models.
- Target: healthcare worker hashtags, UK nurse content, visa content creators.

### LinkedIn
- Use Ad 7A and Ad 7D. More professional tone. Target: nurses, healthcare professionals, location UK, originally from target countries.

### Community-Specific
- The localised ads (7J variants) should run as separate campaigns with community-specific targeting. Nigerian nurses UK Facebook groups, Filipino healthcare worker communities, etc.
- These are the highest-converting ads because they speak directly to the person's identity and situation.
