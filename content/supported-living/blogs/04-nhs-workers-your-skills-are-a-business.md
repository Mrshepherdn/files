## You Already Have the Most Valuable Thing

The biggest myth in business is that you need business experience to start one. You don't. What you need is **domain expertise** — deep knowledge of the work, the service, the people, and the standards.

You have that.

If you're a nurse, a support worker, a healthcare assistant, a care coordinator, or a team leader in the NHS or private care, you already understand:

- How to deliver person-centred care
- How to manage a team and a rota
- How CQC regulation works in practice
- How safeguarding processes operate
- How medication management is handled
- How care plans are written and reviewed
- How to work with multi-disciplinary teams

This knowledge is **worth far more than your salary suggests.** In the private care sector — particularly in supported living — this expertise is the foundation of a business worth hundreds of thousands of pounds in annual revenue.

## The Gap Between You and Business Ownership

So if you already have the skills, what's actually stopping you? Usually it's three things:

### 1. "I don't know the business side"
You know how to deliver care, but you've never registered a company, written a business plan, built financial projections, or set up operational infrastructure.

### 2. "I can't afford it"
You're living on an NHS salary. You don't have £20,000-£50,000 sitting around to start a business from scratch or buy a franchise.

### 3. "The regulatory stuff feels overwhelming"
Ironically, you work within CQC-regulated services every day — but the idea of setting up your own regulated service feels like a different thing entirely.

**All three of these barriers have solutions. And none of them require you to spend years preparing.**
