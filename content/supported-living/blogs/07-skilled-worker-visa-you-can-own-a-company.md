## The Law Is Clear: You Can Own a UK Company

Under UK company law, **any person of any nationality can own shares in a UK limited company.** There is no citizenship requirement. There is no residency requirement. There is no immigration status requirement.

If you are on a Skilled Worker visa — or any visa — you can:

- **Own shares** in a UK limited company
- **Be appointed as a director** of a UK limited company
- **Receive dividends** from that company
- **Hire staff** to work in that company — including a Registered Manager

This is not a loophole. This is not a grey area. **This is standard UK company law.** Companies House does not ask about your immigration status when you register as a shareholder or director. The right to own property — including shares in a company — is not restricted by visa type.

## What You CAN Do — Three Models

### Model 1: Own the Company and Don't Work in It

You purchase a supported living venture. You are registered as a shareholder and director at Companies House. You hire a Registered Manager and support staff to run the day-to-day operations.

You **do not work in the company.** You oversee it as an owner and director — attending governance meetings, reviewing financials, making strategic decisions — but you don't deliver care, manage staff shifts, or do any operational work.

**This is perfectly legal on a Skilled Worker visa.** Owning shares and receiving dividends is not "work" under immigration law. Being a director who oversees governance — without performing operational work — is ownership, not employment.

You keep your sponsored job. You own a business on the side. The business pays a manager, pays its staff, and pays you dividends.

### Model 2: Own the Company and Work in It (Up to 20 Hours Per Week)

Skilled Worker visa holders are permitted to do **supplementary employment** of up to 20 hours per week, provided:

- It's outside the hours of your sponsored employment
- It's in the same occupation code as your sponsored job, OR on the Shortage Occupation List
- You are not replacing your sponsored employment with it

Healthcare and care roles are widely represented on the Shortage Occupation List. If your sponsored role is in healthcare and your business operates in healthcare (such as supported living), working in your own company for up to 20 hours per week may qualify as supplementary employment.

**This means you could:**
- Work your sponsored job during the week
- Spend 20 hours on your own business — managing operations, overseeing care, building relationships with commissioners
- Earn your visa salary AND business income

### Model 3: Full Transition After ILR

Once you receive **Indefinite Leave to Remain** (typically after 5 years), you have no restrictions on employment or business activity. At that point you can:

- Leave your sponsored employment
- Work full-time in your own company
- Expand to multiple businesses
- Do whatever you like — you have the same rights as a British citizen in terms of work

Many Skilled Worker visa holders use Models 1 or 2 **now** — building the business while on their visa — and then transition to full-time ownership after ILR.
