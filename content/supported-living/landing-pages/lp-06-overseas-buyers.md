## Section 1: The UK Is Open for Business

Many people believe you need to be a UK resident — or even a UK citizen — to own a UK company. **This is not true.**

Under UK company law:
- Any person of any nationality can own shares in a UK limited company
- There is no residency requirement for shareholders
- Directors can be based anywhere in the world (at least one must be a natural person)
- Companies House registration is public and transparent

Since December 2020, Skilled Worker visa restrictions no longer prevent visa holders from owning a business either. The UK genuinely welcomes international business ownership.

## Section 8: FAQ

**Do I need a UK bank account?**
The company will need a UK business bank account for operations. This can be opened with your Companies House registration. Several UK banks offer accounts to companies with non-resident directors.

**Can I visit the UK to check on my business?**
Yes. Standard visitor visas allow business meetings, governance oversight, and operational reviews. You do not need a work visa to oversee a company you own.

**What about UK tax?**
The company pays UK corporation tax on profits. Dividend payments to overseas shareholders may be subject to withholding tax or treaty relief depending on your country of residence. Professional tax advice is recommended.

**Can I own more than one venture?**
Absolutely. Portfolio ownership is encouraged for investors seeking scale.

**Is supported living the only sector available?**
No. Zundara offers ventures across multiple healthcare sub-sectors and other industries. Supported living is one of the most in-demand.

**How do I find a Registered Manager?**
The UK care sector has a pool of experienced Registered Managers. Your HR documentation includes job descriptions and recruitment procedures. We can advise on recruitment channels.
