## Section 8: FAQ

**Do I need care qualifications?**
You don't need to be the Registered Manager yourself. You can hire one. But if you have Level 3 or Level 5 in Health & Social Care, you may be eligible to register with CQC directly.

**Do I need experience?**
The policies and procedures guide you through every aspect of running the service. If you already have care experience, you're ahead of most business owners. If you don't, the framework teaches you.

**What about CQC registration?**
CQC registration is a separate process from owning the company. Your venture comes with CQC-aligned policies and procedures. We can guide you through the registration process.

**Can I keep my current job while I set up?**
Yes. Many people set up their company while still employed and transition once the business is generating revenue.

**What if I want to do this with a friend or colleague?**
Group ownership is fully supported. The venture includes governance documentation for shared ownership.
