## Section 3: Everyone Brings Something Different

The best business partnerships aren't three people who are identical. They're people who complement each other.

**The Care Expert** — Knows the clients, understands the service, has hands-on care experience. Leads on quality, care planning, and staff training.

**The Organiser** — Keeps things running. Handles recruitment, scheduling, administration, and compliance. Follows procedures and makes sure nothing falls through the cracks.

**The Connector** — Builds relationships. Talks to local authorities, housing providers, families, and referral sources. Brings in the clients and the contracts.

Or maybe you all have care experience and you share everything equally. The framework supports both models. The policies don't care who follows them — just that they're followed.

## Section 4: Structured, Not Informal

This isn't a handshake. It's a **properly structured business.**

Your venture comes with governance documentation that defines:

- **Share allocation** — who owns what, formally registered at Companies House
- **Director appointments** — legal responsibilities clearly assigned
- **Decision-making** — how decisions are made (majority vote, unanimous, delegated authority)
- **Roles and responsibilities** — who does what in the day-to-day
- **Dividend policy** — how profits are distributed
- **Exit provisions** — what happens if someone wants to leave

Everything is documented. Everything is fair. Everyone knows where they stand from day one.

## Section 8: FAQ

**What if we disagree on something?**
Your governance documents define decision-making processes — majority vote, delegated authority for operational matters, unanimous agreement for major decisions. Disagreements are managed through structure, not emotion.

**What if someone wants to leave?**
Exit provisions are built into the governance framework. Share transfer mechanisms, valuation methods, and notice periods are all defined before you start.

**Do we all need to work in the business?**
No. Some partners may be working owners; others may be investors who hire staff to manage. The structure supports both.

**Can we add more people later?**
Yes. Additional shares can be issued and new directors appointed. The company structure is flexible.

**What if only one of us has care experience?**
That's fine — and actually common. The policies and procedures guide everyone. Care experience is valuable but not a requirement for all owners.
