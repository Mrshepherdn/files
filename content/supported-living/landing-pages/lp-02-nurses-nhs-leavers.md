## Section 8: FAQ

**Can I be the Registered Manager?**
If you hold Level 5 in Leadership for Health and Social Care (or equivalent) and can demonstrate management competence, yes. Many nurses qualify. Alternatively, hire a Registered Manager and operate as owner-director.

**Is supported living different from what I do now?**
If you work in community nursing, learning disability services, or mental health, it's very familiar. If you're in acute hospital care, the principles are the same but the setting is different — and many nurses find it more rewarding.

**What if I've never run a business?**
The policies and procedures run the business. The financial framework manages the money. You bring the clinical leadership and care quality. The rest is built for you.

**Can I still do clinical work?**
Absolutely. Many owner-operators maintain a clinical role within their service, particularly in the early stages. You can be as hands-on or as strategic as you choose.

**What's the realistic timeline to income?**
This varies, but with CQC registration and commissioner engagement, many services achieve their first placement within 3-6 months of commencing the registration process. The financial projections model this realistically.
