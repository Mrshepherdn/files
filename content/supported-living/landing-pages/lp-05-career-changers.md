## Section 3: Why Supported Living?

You might be thinking: "Why would I go into care when I've never worked in care?"

Because supported living has something most industries don't:

- **Guaranteed demand** — local authorities are desperately short of providers
- **Public funding** — revenue comes from government commissioning, not from chasing customers
- **Clear frameworks** — CQC regulation tells you exactly what "good" looks like
- **Social impact** — you're helping people live independently in their own homes
- **Scalability** — one service can grow into a portfolio

This isn't a speculative startup. It's a **proven business model** in a **structurally growing market** with **publicly funded revenue.**
