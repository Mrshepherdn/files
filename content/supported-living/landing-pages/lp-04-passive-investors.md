## Section 1: The Investment Thesis

Supported living is one of the UK's most resilient business models:

- **Revenue source:** Local authority and NHS commissioning — public money, contract-based, recurring
- **Demand driver:** Ageing population, deinstitutionalisation policy, provider shortage
- **Market direction:** Structural growth — demand is increasing faster than supply
- **Regulatory moat:** CQC regulation creates barriers to entry for unprepared competitors
- **Recession resistance:** Publicly funded care is not discretionary spending

This is not a trend. It's a **demographic and policy certainty.** The UK will need more supported living providers every year for the foreseeable future.

## Section 2: The Model — Passive Ownership

You don't need to work in the business. You need to **own** it and **staff** it.

The operating model for passive investors:

1. **Purchase the venture** — company registered, shares transferred, all documentation delivered
2. **Hire a Registered Manager** — experienced professional who handles day-to-day operations and CQC registration
3. **Fund the launch** — use the business plan to secure external funding or deploy personal capital
4. **Oversee governance** — attend quarterly reviews, monitor financials, make strategic decisions
5. **Receive dividends** — profits distributed according to your shareholding

The policies and procedures framework means the business operates to a defined standard regardless of who owns it. The Registered Manager follows the framework. You monitor the results.

## Section 8: FAQ

**Do I need healthcare experience?**
No. As an investor, you hire experienced operational staff. The Registered Manager requirement is met through employment, not ownership.

**How hands-on do I need to be?**
As little or as much as you choose. Minimum involvement: quarterly governance reviews and financial oversight. The framework runs the operations.

**Can I own multiple ventures?**
Yes. Many investors purchase multiple ventures to build a portfolio. Volume pricing may apply.

**What's the exit strategy?**
You own a UK limited company with an established revenue stream, client base, and operational framework. This is a sellable asset. Care businesses with CQC registration and active contracts command premium valuations.

**Can I invest from overseas?**
Yes. There is no UK residency requirement for company ownership. The business can be operated by a UK-based Registered Manager while you oversee remotely.

**What ongoing costs should I expect?**
Staff salaries (your largest cost), premises, insurance, and optional infrastructure support (£49-£99/month). All costs are modelled in the financial projections.
